# Community code of conduct

## Our standard

We want a technically rigorous, patient, and welcoming community. Be respectful, assume good faith
while checking evidence, explain disagreements concretely, accept correction, and make room for
people with different experience levels, languages, family structures, and accessibility needs.

Examples of constructive participation include:

- reviewing code and claims rather than attacking people;
- separating measured facts from assumptions and model inference;
- giving reproducible feedback without exposing household data;
- crediting upstream work and respecting license boundaries;
- stopping a risky change when privacy, consent, or authority is unclear.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, insults, sexualized attention, discrimination,
deliberate disruption, impersonation, retaliation, or publishing another person's private
information. It also includes pressuring contributors to share family media, credentials, location,
network topology, or other sensitive evidence.

## Scope

This code applies in project repositories, reviews, issues, discussions, chats, events, and other
spaces where a person is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or reject contributions, issue a private warning,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant. Enforcement should be
proportionate, documented privately, consistent, and designed to protect affected people from
further contact or retaliation.

Report conduct concerns through a private maintainer channel. If none is published, open a
content-free request for private contact without naming people or describing the incident publicly.
Security or privacy incidents must also follow `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers involved in a report must
recuse themselves from deciding it when another maintainer is available.
